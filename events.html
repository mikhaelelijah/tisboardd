No Backpack Day

No Backpack Day is an event where students come to school without using their regular school bags. Students can carry their books and learning supplies in creative and unique ways, such as using shopping bags, buckets, suitcases, or even making their own handmade carriers. Students who create their own crafts during No Backpack Day will be evaluated, and the top ten most creative crafts will be selected and given special appreciation. This activity aims to foster creativity and create a more exciting and fun learning atmosphere.
